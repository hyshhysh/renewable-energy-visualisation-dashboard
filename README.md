### Energy Nexus: Mapping the Renewable Future

Energy Nexus is an interactive Plotly Dash application designed to explore global renewable energy trends, investments, affordability, and the role of activism. 
Through engaging visualizations and data storytelling, it empowers stakeholders—from policymakers to students—to understand the multifaceted dynamics of renewable energy adoption.

>*The original project was a final group assignment for my degree module "Data Visualisation and Simulation".*

###Features
- Interactive Dash visualisations across 6 hypotheses
- Global Datasets: renewable shares, fossil fuel reliance, investment trends, LCOE, activism data
- Hypothesis-based storytelling to guide users through insights
- Visual elements: bar charts, line charts, scatter plots, radar charts, Sankey diagrams, heatmaps, and causal loop diagrams

---

### Hypotheses
1. Correlation between energy imports and renewable energy share
2. Investment and innovation as drivers of clean energy transition
3. Affordability and energy mix: Global cost trends
4. Solar energy leadership: Capacity and consumption by region
5. Diversification to reduce fossil dependence
6. Activism and civic engagement shaping green policies

---

### How to Run
1. Clone the repository
2. Install dependencies (plotly dash)
3. Run `app.py`
4. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8050` in browser.

---

### Data Sources
All datasets are stored locally in the `data/` directory. Sources include:
Our World in Data
IEA
Ember Climate
EEB
Protest and tweet datasets for civic engagement
